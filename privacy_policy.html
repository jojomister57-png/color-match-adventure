Privacy Policy

Color Match Adventure (Android)

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Color Match Adventure (the “App”), published on Google Play by Jojo Mister (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the App on Android devices.

1. Summary

  • The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • The App works fully offline. It does not request internet access or any other Android permission.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, social or payment SDKs, and no in-app purchases.
  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. The App does not ask for, read or receive your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal information. We operate no servers that receive data from the App.

3. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the App keeps a small amount of game data in its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, best star ratings for each level, and which tutorial hints you have already seen.
  • Rewards: your Gold Coins balance, the date of your last daily reward (taken from your device’s local calendar date), daily reward and treasure chest status.
  • Settings: music and sound on/off and their volume levels.

This data is created and used only by the App on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. It does not identify you.

4. How the data is used

The data described in section 3 is used only to run the game: to remember your progress, rewards and settings between sessions.

5. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any information to third parties, because the App does not collect or transmit any information.

6. Third-party services and libraries

The App is built with the open-source Godot Engine and standard Android support libraries (AndroidX, Kotlin standard library). In this App they do not collect or transmit personal data. The App does not include Google Play Services, Firebase, advertising networks, analytics or attribution tools.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information in connection with downloading and updating apps and, if you have allowed it in your device settings, crash and diagnostic information, which Google may share with developers in aggregated form. This processing is performed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by the App.

7. Link to this policy

The App’s Settings screen contains a “PRIVACY POLICY” button that opens this page in your web browser. The App itself sends no data when you do this. The website that hosts this page is run by a hosting provider, which may process standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page, as with any website.

8. Data security

Your game data stays in the App’s private storage on your device, protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Android cloud backup is disabled for the App, so the data is not copied to any backup service by the App. Because nothing is transmitted, no data is exposed in transit.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by either:

  • uninstalling the App, or
  • opening Android Settings → Apps → Color Match Adventure → Storage → Clear storage (the exact menu names depend on your device).

We hold no copies of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

10. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The App contains no advertising, no purchases and no way to communicate with other people.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect or hold any personal data, we have no data about you to provide, correct or delete. Your on-device game data can be removed at any time as described in section 9. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this Privacy Policy and change the effective date above before the changed version of the App is released. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App’s update notes.
